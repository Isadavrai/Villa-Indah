## 1. Executive direction

The central booking objection is ambiguity between **private residence** and **hotel/resort amenities**. Lead with ownership of the entire villa, privacy, 4 bedrooms / 8 guests, private pool, then explain precisely which shared resort services are included, chargeable, or subject to rules. The second objection is booking security: rates, total cost, confirmation, payments, cancellation, damages/deposit, guest capacity. Use concise guest-first prose; do not paste policies from competitors.

### A — The Villa (5)

**Q1. Is Villa Indah a private villa or part of the hotel?**  
Villa Indah is a private four-bedroom residence located within the Karma Kandara resort grounds in Bali. You enjoy your own villa and private pool, with selected resort facilities available according to the applicable access conditions.

**Q2. Will we share the villa with other guests?**  
No. When you book Villa Indah, the residence is reserved exclusively for your group. Shared resort areas are separate from your private villa.

**Q3. How many people can stay at Villa Indah?**  
The villa accommodates up to eight guests across four air-conditioned bedrooms. Please include adults and children in your enquiry so we can confirm the most suitable sleeping arrangement.

**Q4. Does Villa Indah have its own swimming pool?**  
Yes. Villa Indah has its own private swimming pool. The resort also has shared pool facilities; access to those areas is governed by resort arrangements.

**Q5. What makes the villa different from a standard hotel suite?**  
You have the space and privacy of a complete four-bedroom residence, including your own pool, while staying within an established cliffside resort destination.

### B — Resort & Beach (5)

**Q6. Can Villa Indah guests use Karma Kandara facilities? [VERIFY]**  
Villa Indah is located within Karma Kandara, which offers restaurants, spa facilities, pools and Karma Beach. Access, reservation requirements and charges may vary by facility. Please ask us for the current list of inclusions before booking.

**Q7. Is beach access included? [VERIFY]**  
Beach access is part of the villa stay arrangements as currently described by the property. Beach club seating, food and beverages, and certain services may be charged separately. We will confirm the applicable conditions for your dates before you book.

**Q8. Are sun loungers and beach club services free? [VERIFY]**  
Beach loungers and beach club services are not automatically included in the villa rate. Any applicable charges or minimum spends will be confirmed before your visit.

**Q9. How do guests get down to Karma Beach? [VERIFY]**  
Karma Beach is located below the cliffs. The resort manages beach access arrangements, which may change for operational or weather-related reasons. Please contact us for the current route and accessibility details.

**Q10. Are resort restaurants and spa treatments included? [VERIFY]**  
Karma Kandara offers dining and wellness facilities, but meals, spa treatments and other resort services should be considered separately charged unless specifically included in your written booking offer.

### C — Services & Stay (4)

**Q11. Is housekeeping included? [VERIFY]**  
Housekeeping arrangements depend on your confirmed booking package. We will specify the cleaning frequency and linen/towel service in your reservation details.

**Q12. Can breakfast or a private chef be arranged? [VERIFY]**  
Please tell us what dining experience you are looking for. We can clarify the available options and any additional costs before confirming your stay.

**Q13. Can you arrange airport transfers? [VERIFY]**  
Let us know your flight details and number of travellers. We will advise on available transfer arrangements and costs.

**Q14. What time are check-in and check-out? [VERIFY]**  
Your confirmed booking details will state the check-in and check-out times. Early arrival or late departure requests are subject to availability and may incur an extra charge.

### D — Booking & Payment (5)

**Q15. How do I check availability and book?**  
Send us your preferred dates, number of guests and contact details through the website enquiry form or WhatsApp. We will reply with availability, a personalised quotation and the terms required to confirm your reservation.

**Q16. Is the advertised price for the whole villa?**  
Villa Indah is offered as a complete private residence, not as individual rooms. Your quotation will confirm the total rental price for your dates and guest count.

**Q17. Are taxes, service charges and resort fees included? [VERIFY]**  
We provide an itemised quotation showing the rental price and any applicable taxes, service charges or optional extras before you commit to a booking.

**Q18. Is a deposit required and how can I pay? [VERIFY]**  
If a deposit or advance payment is required, the amount, payment schedule and accepted methods will be detailed in your booking offer. Only use the payment instructions provided in your written confirmation.

**Q19. What is the cancellation policy? [VERIFY]**  
Cancellation and refund conditions will be shown in writing before payment. The applicable policy may depend on your travel dates, season and confirmed rate.

### E — House Rules & Special Requests (3)

**Q20. Can we bring children? [VERIFY]**  
Families are welcome to enquire. Please share the ages of any children so we can confirm bedding options, relevant safety information and any resort restrictions. Children require adult supervision around pools, cliffs and the beach.

**Q21. Can we host a celebration, event or outside visitors? [VERIFY]**  
Please tell us about any planned gathering or visitors before booking. Events and additional guests may require advance written approval and can be subject to resort rules and extra charges.

**Q22. Are pets and smoking permitted? [VERIFY]**  
Please ask us about the current pet and smoking policies before booking. Conditions may apply both to the private villa and to shared resort areas.

- Use the site's existing translations for English, Russian, and the third supported language discovered from repo settings. Do not assume the third language; discover it.
- Do not auto-translate or overwrite unrelated live content. Add translations for every Q&A; preserve brand, Karma Kandara and Karma Beach names.

- Track optional events (`faq_open`, `faq_contact_click`) only if the site already has a properly configured analytics layer and consent handling. Don't inject Google Analytics/Meta Pixel without explicit approval.
