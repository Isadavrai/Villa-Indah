# Villa Indah: reels from **real property photos** (RU / EN)

## Outputs
The [GitHub Actions renderer](../.github/workflows/render-villa-reels.yml) uses the actual photos in `assets/photos-new/` to make two silent 9:16 drafts for review:
- `villa_indah_ru_vertical_silent.mp4`
- `villa_indah_en_vertical_silent.mp4`

**Specs:** 1080 × 1920, ~16 seconds, six shots, crossfade transitions, original room/pool/beach images, no AI-generated hotel scenes, no implied private ownership of shared resort facilities. A soundtrack is intentionally not bundled to avoid music licensing problems; add platform-approved audio when publishing.

Reels have no fake availability, prices or reviews. Posts should direct guests to the live enquiry form / business DM after verifying these channels. Treat a visit to the site as a lead, **not** a confirmed booking.

## Additional content directions
1. **Villa walkthrough:** pool → living room → bedrooms → grounds → booking CTA
2. **Group escape:** 4 bedrooms, comfortable shared spaces, private pool, up to 8 guests
3. **Resort access:** villa vs. shared resort areas clearly labeled. Never claim shared beach or cliff-top pool is part of the private villa.
4. **Photo details:** individual room slides, quick tour, short descriptions. No made-up amenities.
5. **Seasonal planning:** use actual calendar and verified guest availability only after the property operator confirms them.

## Performance checklist
Track video plays, profile clicks, website enquiry form completions, qualified DM requests, **confirmed guest booking revenue and payout actually received**. Do not mix this project with editing services for other clients.
